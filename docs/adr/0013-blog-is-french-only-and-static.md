# The blog is French only, and its listing is pre-rendered pages

The blog exists for search engines, not for the people who book a session, and the business writes it in French only. So it breaks three rules the rest of the site holds, on purpose.

**No `en` route, no internationalized fields.** `blogPage` and `blogArticle` carry plain `string` and `text` fields, the Query Module hard-codes `fr`, and the services take no `lang`. `getRoutesForLang("en")` has no `blog` key at all — the footer narrows with `"blog" in routes` — rather than an English route that would render French. The alternative, internationalized arrays filled with the French value twice, was rejected: it is the plumbing of [ADR 0003](./0003-content-localization.md) paid for a language that will never be authored, and every `en` query on the site would still have to be told to skip it.

**Pagination is a route, not a script.** `/blog/` is page 1, `/blog/page/N` the rest, twelve articles each, generated by `getStaticPaths` from the same fetch. Robots get small pages linked by real `<a>`s and `rel=prev/next`; no JavaScript, no query string, no client-side state. One page holding every article was rejected because it grows without bound and the pagination control the design draws would have nothing to do. Search, sort and venue filter were drawn too and are deliberately not built: every one of them needs either a route matrix or a script, and neither serves a robot.

**The route fetches.** `<title>`, `<meta description>` and `rel=prev/next` come from the CMS and belong to the `<head>`, which is the Layout's, which only the route can feed — a Page cannot fill a Layout slot from below. So `blog/index.astro` and `blog/page/[page].astro` call `getBlogPageData` themselves and hand the result to `BlogPage`, which composes and no longer fetches. Every other route still holds no data.

## Consequences

- The blog listing has no Page Cover and no Page Component array: a badge from the locales, a title and an intro authored on the `blogPage` singleton. It is the one page outside the "cover + components" shape of `CONTEXT.md`.
- The two newest articles are drawn above the grid on page 1 only, and the grid still lists them: the top row is a showcase, not a partition, so page counts never depend on it.
- The article page is not built. Cards link to `/blog/[slug]`, which 404s until it is.
- `Layout` grew `title` and `description` props and a `head` slot; every other route still renders the placeholder title.
